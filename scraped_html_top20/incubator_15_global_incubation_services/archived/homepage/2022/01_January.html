Our Programs

Thoughtfully designed programs to help every aspect of your Start-up. We have something for every stage of entrepreneurship.

Pivot Labs

Turnaround your startup’s fortune in 21 Days. A deep dive program with accomplished entrepreneurs and angel investors to consolidate your IP, re-evaluate your strategy and rediscover purpose.

Ginceralator

An unique data-driven acceleration program with an intensive, hands-on3-month exercise ensuring tech startups are on a fast paced growth trajectory. Mentored by the best minds, helps in raising the seed fund and angel investment.

GINCap

Raising capital is an important milestone in an entrepreneur’s journey. GINcap will demystify the science and methodologies behind successful fund raising to propel the startups to next level of growth.

LaunchLift

The smart way a startup launches its product or service decides its wins in the markets. The best strategic marketing minds will help the entrepreneur define the most optimized, smart way of creating an impactful market entry.

STRATUp

The ability of a Startup to foresee and mitigate risks can be one of the biggest factors for its success. So, how does one do it? Get all the answers with this deep dive program, with the best business strategists coaching you.

WE Power

Woman Entrepreneurships brings its own challenges. WE Power provides a platform to be part of a workshop with such woman leaders who have successfully traversed this path can reveal the strategies and answers. Join them!
