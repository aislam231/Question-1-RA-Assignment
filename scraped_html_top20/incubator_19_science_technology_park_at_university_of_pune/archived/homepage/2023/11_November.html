Skill and Entrepreneurship Development
Scitech Park has a state - of - art, modern infrastructure for conducting various training programs in the field of Technology Entrepreneurship.
Business Incubation
The main objective of the Park is to support new, promising and innovative technology based startup companies in the areas of IT/ITES, Clean Technology, Renewable Energy, Agriculture and Food Technology, Pharma, Biotechnology, Electronics and Telecommunication, Cyber Security, Social Incubation etc.
CSR
The CSR activities of Scitech Park are aligned with the objective of the trust and the applicability of the Companies Act 2013.
Our Focus Areas

Scitech Park, Pune established independent Business Incubator, "Growth Lab" at Bhosari in 2006-07 with just 4 start-ups. So far through its incubation program, the Park has groomed 176 startups. These companies are from 14 different technology sectors with simple data entry operators to DNA based diagnostics.
